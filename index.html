The Problem

Faceless content is booming, but most AI video tools rely on stock footage, templates, or stiff avatars. The result? Videos that look automated and lack originality.

The Solution

GraphMotion generates videos as code-driven motion graphics—charts, diagrams, and illustrated scenes—tailored to your prompt. No shared libraries, no templates, just unique, animated content.

How It Works

  1. Submit a Prompt: Describe the video you want—topic, tone, or style direction.
  2. AI Generates Code: GraphMotion writes and renders original animation code (Motion Canvas-based) for your video.
  3. Review & Edit: Preview the render and request targeted edits in plain language (e.g., "Make this scene blue").
  4. Export & Post: Download your finished video, ready to share on any platform.
