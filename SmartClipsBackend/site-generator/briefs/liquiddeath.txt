Company: Liquid Death (Supplying Demand, Inc.). Los Angeles, founded 2017, first cans shipped January 2019. Founder & CEO Mike Cessario, a former advertising creative director.
Tagline: "Murder Your Thirst."
What we do: we make health and sustainability 50 times more fun. Premium low-calorie drinks in infinitely recyclable aluminum tallboy cans. An entertainment company that happens to sell beverages, using dark comedy, heavy-metal and punk culture to compete with the fun branding of energy drinks, beer and junk food. Part of proceeds fights plastic pollution ("Death to Plastic").
Products: Mountain Water (still and sparkling, natural mountain springs); Soda-flavored sparkling water (Severed Lime, Mango Chainsaw, Berry It Alive, Convicted Melon; low calorie, no artificial sweeteners); Iced Tea (low sugar, B vitamins); Death Dust electrolyte powder sticks; Sparkling Energy launched Jan 2026 (about 100 mg caffeine, zero sugar; flavors Orange Horror, Tropical Terror, Scary Strawberry, Murder Mystery).
Audience: Gen Z and millennials who love humor, alternative / punk / metal culture, sustainability and cleaner ingredients; festival, skate, tattoo and outdoor crowds plus mainstream shoppers.
Scale: about 333 million dollars retail revenue in 2024, 1.4 billion dollar valuation, 100,000+ retail doors (Walmart, Target, Whole Foods, 7-Eleven, Amazon, Live Nation venues), about 370 employees.
Personality: irreverent, darkly humorous, anti-corporate, radically transparent, entertainment-first.
Visual identity: black and white, monochrome, heavy-metal and gothic, skull motifs, high-contrast, dark and bold, never bright or pastel.
