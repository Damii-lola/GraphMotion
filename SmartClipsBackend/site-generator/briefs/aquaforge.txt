Company: AquaForge Beverages. Functional beverages born in the Rocky Mountain foothills (Boulder, Colorado). Founded in 2018 by Maya Chen (product lead) and Jordan Hale (food scientist), who got tired of the beverage industry's "ingredient theater".
Tagline: "Forge Your Flow. Pure Energy, Zero Compromise."
Promise: clean, science-backed hydration and energy. Zero artificial anything. The company publishes its lab results - most brands hope you never ask. Sold in about 4,200 stores. Cans are 100% recyclable.
Product lines:
- AquaForge Core - everyday hydration: still and lightly sparkling water with electrolytes and trace minerals. Flavors: Citrus Grove, Cucumber Mint, Wild Berry, Unflavored. 0g sugar, 10-20 calories.
- AquaForge Charge - functional energy: 80-120mg natural caffeine from green tea and guarana, plus rhodiola, ashwagandha and B-vitamins. Built for people who train. No artificial anything, no crash.
- AquaForge Recover - post-activity: electrolytes plus plant-based protein isolate, vegan, in Chocolate Sea Salt, Vanilla Chai and Strawberry Basil.
Audience: active, health-conscious adults - runners, climbers, gym-goers, people who read labels.
Personality: clean, honest, scientific but warm, premium without pretension. Mountain water, sunrise energy, calm recovery.
Visual identity: Forge Deep navy #0B1C2D, Aqua Core teal #00A8A8, Clean White #F8FAFB, Charge Orange #FF6B35, Recover Sage #7BA05B, Mineral Gray #6B7C8A.
